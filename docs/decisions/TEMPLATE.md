# NNNN — Short decision title

**Status:** proposed | survives audit | revised | open question

## The claim
What you're deciding, stated as a testable assertion, not a vague direction.
(e.g. "Tenure should be weighted positively — longer tenure protects an employee's score" is testable.
"Tenure matters" is not.)

## Reasoning
Why you're making this call. What evidence, source, or logic backs it. If it's a judgment call
with no hard evidence, say so explicitly rather than dressing it up as data-driven.

## Adversarial audit
Claude's job here is to try to break the claim, not confirm it: counter-arguments, missing evidence,
alternative interpretations of the same data, what a skeptical interviewer would push on.

## Verdict
- **Survives** — reasoning holds, no changes needed.
- **Revised** — here's what changed and why.
- **Open question** — genuinely unresolved, flagged rather than papered over.
